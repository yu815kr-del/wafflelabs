2. Data kept only on your device

  • Date of birth is used on-device only to check you are 18+; it is never stored or sent. Only the result (and, if under 18, a 24-hour lockout time) is kept on the device.
  • Region eligibility is checked on-device from your Google Play country (or SIM country). We do not use IP address or location, and the result is not sent. The app includes the Google Play Billing library solely to read the Play country code; there are no in-app purchases or payments.
  • Recent chats list, counters, display settings, remaining undos.

3. Video and audio

  • Video and audio go directly between the two devices (peer-to-peer, WebRTC); they do not pass through or get stored or recorded on our servers. They are encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP).
  • We use Google’s public STUN servers (stun.l.google.com, stun1.l.google.com) to establish the connection. Your IP address is visible to the STUN server (Google) and to your chat partner. Signaling data in section 1 also includes IP addresses.
  • Camera/microphone permission is requested only when you first start a video chat.
  • The roughly 1.5-second cover after connecting is not content analysis; the app does not analyze video.

4. Visible to others

Your chat partner sees your nickname, country, language, interests, your video/audio (video mode) or messages (text mode), and your IP address via signaling. Reports are never shown to the reported user and are readable only by us.

6. Your rights and deletion

  • Settings → Delete account immediately deletes your profile, block list, queue entry, and Firebase sign-in account. Recent chats and history on this device are also removed.
  • Chat records, text messages, signaling data, and reports remain for the other participant and for safety review. Intended periods are in the table above, but auto-deletion may be off as described in section 1.
  • If you do not have the app, follow the account deletion page or email yu815kr@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction.
  • Data kept only on the device is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Children

GlobeChat is for adults 18+ only. Users who appear to be minors can be reported (“Looks under 18”).

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS, WebRTC DTLS-SRTP) and Firestore security rules so that only you and your chat partner can read or write the relevant documents. Reports cannot be read in the app. We cannot guarantee absolute security of internet transmission.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app or the store listing.
