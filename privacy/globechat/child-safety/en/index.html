Child Safety Standards

App: GlobeChat · Package: com.wafflelabs.globechat
Operator: WaffleLabs
Effective date: 2026-11-14
URL: https://yu815kr-del.github.io/wafflelabs/privacy/globechat/child-safety/

These are WaffleLabs’ published standards for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in GlobeChat. They are posted to meet Google Play’s Child Safety Standards and describe only features the app actually provides and actions we take as the operator.

1. Age requirement

  • GlobeChat is for adults 18 and older only.
  • When you first use the app, we check your age from the date of birth you enter. This check happens on your device only; the date of birth itself is never stored or sent to our servers. If you are under 18, you cannot use the app, and that device cannot try again for 24 hours.
  • Users who appear to be minors can be reported in the app with the reason “Looks like a minor.”

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

GlobeChat does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation. The following are prohibited with zero tolerance:

  • Use of the app by anyone under 18, or lying about age to use it
  • Any sexual content, language, requests, or grooming directed at a minor
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): creating, possessing, sending, or sharing any visual depiction — including photos, videos, and computer-generated imagery — of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct
  • Sexual exploitation of a child, or related trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation

If this conduct is confirmed or clearly suspected, we will take appropriate action, including disabling the account, and will report it to the authorities listed below.

3. In-app reporting and blocking

You can report or block someone without leaving the app.

  • Where to report: the Report control during a video chat; More → Report in a text chat; and Report in Recent chats (for chats in the last 24 hours).
  • Report reasons: Nudity / Sexual talk or behavior / Abuse or harassment / Looks like a minor / Spam or ads / Face isn’t visible / Other. You may add a note of up to 300 characters.
  • Right after a report: the chat ends immediately and that person is blocked. We do not tell the reported person about the report. This cannot be undone.
  • Blocking: separately from reporting, you can choose “Block and next” during a chat or “Block” in Recent chats. You will not be matched with someone you have blocked. You can unblock them in Settings → Block list.
  • What a report stores: the reporter’s and target’s account identifiers, the chat ID, the reason, any note, and the time. Reports cannot be read in the app; only we can review them. The intended retention period is 365 days.

4. How we handle reports

  • We review reports that are submitted.
  • Video and audio travel directly between the two devices (peer-to-peer, WebRTC). They do not pass through or get stored or recorded on our servers, and the app does not analyze video content. We therefore cannot replay the video itself. Review is based on the report reason and note, and on stored chat records and text messages.
  • If CSAE or CSAM, or use by a minor, is confirmed or clearly suspected, we take appropriate action, including disabling the account.
  • Tips sent to yu815kr@gmail.com are reviewed under the same standards.

5. Reporting to authorities

If CSAM or the sexual exploitation of a child is confirmed or clearly suspected, we report it to the relevant authorities under applicable law. This includes:

  • the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC, CyberTipline)
  • the Korean National Police Agency

Where appropriate, we may also notify other law-enforcement or child-protection agencies in the relevant region. You may also report directly to those authorities, or tell us at yu815kr@gmail.com.

6. Child safety point of contact

WaffleLabs · yu815kr@gmail.com

This is the contact for user reports and for notices about CSAE from Google Play or others, so we can review and take action.

7. Related documents and changes

How we process personal data, how long reports are kept, and account deletion are described in the Privacy Policy. If we change these standards, we will post the update here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app or the store listing.
