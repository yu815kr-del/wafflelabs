앱에서 삭제하기

  1. GlobeChat을 열고 Google 계정으로 로그인합니다.
  2. 설정 → 계정 삭제를 고른 뒤, 안내를 확인하고 삭제합니다.

이렇게 하면 내 프로필, 차단 목록, 매칭 대기 문서, Firebase 로그인 계정, 이 기기의 최근 대화·기록이 즉시 삭제됩니다.

앱이 없을 때 (이메일 요청)

앱을 설치할 수 없거나 앱에서 삭제할 수 없으면, yu815kr@gmail.com으로 아래를 적어 보내 주세요.

  • 제목: GlobeChat 계정 삭제 요청 / GlobeChat account deletion request
  • GlobeChat에 로그인한 Google 계정 이메일
  • 삭제 요청이라는 점

운영자는 요청이 본인 것인지 확인한 뒤 처리합니다. 확인을 위해 추가로 정보를 물을 수 있으며, 보통 며칠 안에 결과를 회신합니다.

  • 이 기기에만 있는 최근 대화·기록·설정(앱을 지우면 기기에만 있는 정보도 함께 삭제됩니다)

GlobeChat account deletion

App: GlobeChat · Package: com.wafflelabs.globechat
Operator: WaffleLabs
Effective date: 2026-11-14

Delete in the app

  1. Open GlobeChat and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account, read the notice, and confirm.

This immediately deletes your profile, block list, matching-queue entry, Firebase sign-in account, and recent chats and history on this device.

If you do not have the app (email request)

Email yu815kr@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: GlobeChat account deletion request
  • The Google account email you used to sign in to GlobeChat
  • A clear request to delete the account

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder before we act. We may ask for more information to confirm identity, and we will reply with the result, usually within a few days.

What is deleted immediately

  • Your public profile (nickname, country, language, interests)
  • Your block list
  • Your matching-queue entry
  • Your Firebase Authentication Google sign-in account
  • Recent chats, history, and settings stored only on this device (uninstalling the app also removes on-device data)

What remains

  • Chat records, text messages, signaling data, and reports already submitted remain for the other participant and for safety review.
  • Intended retention is 90 days for chat records and text messages, 1 day for signaling, and 365 days for reports. Firestore time-to-live (TTL) auto-deletion is not currently enabled, so this data may remain until we delete it or turn on auto-deletion.
  • You can email the same address to request earlier deletion.
