Privacy Policy

App: Undam (오늘의 운담) · Package: com.wafflelabs.undam
Operator: WaffleLabs
Effective date: 2026-11-07

The App’s screens are in Korean; Korean menu names are shown in parentheses.

WaffleLabs (“we”) explains below how information is handled in the app “Undam” (the “App”) published on Google Play.

1. Accounts

The App has no sign-up or login, and we do not create or manage user accounts. We do not operate our own server to which the App sends user information.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Profile: nickname (optional), date of birth (solar/lunar), birth time (optional), gender (optional), and birth and residence regions chosen by you from a city list built into the App. The App does not use GPS or the location permission.
  • Fortune, saju, tarot, palm, face, talisman and lotto-number results and archive history; settings such as notifications and theme; the ad-removal purchase check result; and ad-spacing records.

The App does not send this information to us. If Android device backup (Google backup) is on, it may be included in your backup. However, starting with App version 0.4.8, the ad-removal purchase check result and ad-spacing records are stored in a separate file that is excluded from backup and device-to-device transfer, and palm photo files left by earlier versions are also excluded. Backups already created by versions before 0.4.8 may contain these ad records. On a new device, ad removal is re-checked against your Google Play purchase history.

3. Camera and photos (palm and face reading)

  • The camera is used only after you choose to take a photo in palm or face reading, agree to the in-app notice, and allow the Android permission on first use. You can also pick a photo from your gallery.
  • Face and hand positions (point coordinates) are found on your device by Google ML Kit face detection and Google MediaPipe hand landmarks included in the App, and their proportions are used for an entertainment reading. They are not used to recognize or authenticate anyone.
  • The App has no feature that uploads photos to our servers or to the ad SDK. Face photos are kept only in device memory while the result is shown, and palm photos only while the App is running; neither is saved as a file. For palm reading, only the result text and measured ratios (numbers) are stored on the device.
  • However, these two SDKs may send diagnostic and usage information, separate from photos, to Google (section 5).
  • Saving to the gallery and sharing happen only when you choose them, and share cards do not contain photos.

4. Lotto result updates

The App downloads the latest results file over HTTPS, without authentication, from a fixed address on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It does not send your name, date of birth, inputs, advertising ID or cookies. However, as with any internet connection, your IP address, device/OS information (User-Agent) and the request time reach GitHub, and GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not receive or view these logs; their retention follows GitHub’s policies.

About GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages
GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

5. Information processed by Google SDKs

The following is as described in each SDK’s official documentation; processing and retention of this information follow the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

  • Google AdMob (ads) and User Messaging Platform (UMP, consent): Google states that AdMob automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, the IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions such as app launches and taps, app and SDK performance diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, encrypted in transit with TLS (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). UMP checks whether consent is required and stores your choice on the device. The App does not pass your profile, photos or results to the ad SDK. Purchasing ad removal stops ads from being shown. How Google uses information from partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google ML Kit (face detection): Google states that it sends device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version), per-installation identifiers not intended to identify a user or device, performance metrics, API configuration (such as image format and resolution), input and output size, feature version, event type and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics over HTTPS, and does not transfer them to third parties (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).
  • Google MediaPipe (hand landmarks): Google states that input data such as images is processed on the device and not sent to Google servers, and that performance and usage metrics (such as session and inference counts, hardware performance, app ID, general characteristics of processed media, and host system and version) are sent to Google to measure performance, improve the APIs and detect misuse (https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/legal/tos).
  • Google Play: The ad-removal in-app purchase, app update prompts and downloads of the App’s artwork (Play Asset Delivery) are handled through Google Play. We do not receive card or other payment details; only the purchase check result is stored on the device.

6. Notifications

Fortune and lotto notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device; no information is sent to us for them. You can turn them off in the App. The notification permission is used only if you allow it.

7. Purposes

  • Providing App features such as fortune, saju, tarot, palm, face, talisman and lotto-number generation and keeping your history
  • Showing and measuring ads and preventing fraud (AdMob)
  • Verifying in-app purchases (Google Play)
  • Updating lotto results (section 4)
  • Responding to inquiries (if you email us)

8. Sharing with third parties

Other than the services in sections 4 and 5, we do not provide or sell your information to third parties, except where required by law.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Information stored on the device stays there until you delete it. “Delete all local data” (‘모든 로컬 데이터 삭제’) on the “My info” (‘내 정보’) screen deletes your profile, history, settings, palm results and photos held in memory, and “Delete palm photos & results” (‘손금 사진·결과 삭제’) on the palm screen deletes palm data only. Uninstalling the App also deletes the information inside it. Because there are no accounts, there is no separate account-deletion procedure.
  • Information included in Google backup can be managed in your device’s Google backup settings.
  • Images saved to your gallery or shared to other apps must be deleted there.
  • We keep no user data on our servers. Inquiry emails you send us are kept for one year after the inquiry is resolved and then deleted. To request earlier deletion, write to the same address (yu815kr@gmail.com).
  • Retention of information processed by Google or GitHub follows each company’s policies.

10. Advertising ID and personalized ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in device settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Menu names may vary by device and OS version. If UMP determines that consent is required, a consent form is shown when the App starts; if it determines that privacy options must be offered, “Ad privacy settings” (‘광고 개인정보 설정’) appears under “My info” (‘내 정보’) so you can change your choice.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

12. Entertainment notice

All readings and lotto-number generation in the App are for entertainment and reference only and are not medical, legal or investment advice. Lotto-number generation does not guarantee winning, and the App does not sell lottery tickets.

13. Security

We take reasonable safeguards, but cannot guarantee absolute security of transmission over the internet.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post it on this page with its effective date and announce important changes in the App or the store listing.
