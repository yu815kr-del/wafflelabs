Privacy Policy

App: Undam (오늘의 운담) · Package: com.wafflelabs.undam
Operator: WaffleLabs
Effective date: 2026-11-20

The App’s screens are in Korean; Korean menu names are shown in parentheses.

WaffleLabs (“we”) explains below how information is handled in the app “Undam” (the “App”) published on Google Play.

1. Accounts

The App has no sign-up or login, and we do not create or manage user accounts. We do not operate our own server to which the App sends user information.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Profile: nickname (optional), date of birth (solar/lunar), birth time (optional), gender (optional), and birth and residence regions chosen by you from a city list built into the App. The App does not use GPS or the location permission.
  • Fortune, saju, tarot, palm, face, talisman and lotto-number results and archive history; settings such as notifications and theme; the ad-removal purchase check result; and ad-spacing records.
  • Undam Plus subscription status as reported by Google Play (free trial, subscribed, cancellation scheduled, payment pending, not subscribed), purchase time, auto-renew flag, trial length and last check time; a purchase confirmation value and trial length noted when you start a subscription in the App, used only to calculate the trial end date; and the price text shown by Google Play (used to show a “recent price” when the live price cannot be loaded).
  • Whether Remove Ads was purchased before 00:00 KST on November 14, 2026 (yes/no only). It is determined from the purchase time in your Google Play purchase history and used only to keep monthly New Year details, the 12-period daily chart and the custom morning notification for those earlier buyers. The purchase time itself is not stored, and the value is cleared at the next check if the purchase is refunded.
  • The New Year months you unlocked by watching a rewarded ad, morning-notification content choices, hide-until dates for home cards, and whether you already recommended the App.
  • A reduced palm photo and hand-position coordinates kept so the saved palm result screen can show the hand again (see section 3).

The App does not send this information to us. If Android device backup (Google backup) is on, it may be included in your backup. However, starting with App version 0.4.8, the ad-removal purchase check result and ad-spacing records are stored in a separate file that is excluded from backup and device-to-device transfer. The Undam Plus status, purchase confirmation value and price text (from 0.7.0) and the earlier Remove Ads purchase flag (from 0.7.1) are kept in the same file and are also excluded. Palm photos (the reduced copy and hand-position coordinates, including files left by earlier versions) are also excluded from backup and device-to-device transfer. Backups already created by versions before 0.4.8 may contain these ad records. On a new device, Remove Ads and Undam Plus are re-checked against your Google Play purchase history.

3. Camera and photos (palm and face reading)

  • The camera is used only after you choose to take a photo in palm or face reading, agree to the in-app notice, and allow the Android permission on first use. You can also pick a photo from your gallery.
  • Face and hand positions (point coordinates) are found on your device by Google ML Kit face detection and Google MediaPipe hand landmarks included in the App, and their proportions are used for an entertainment reading. They are not used to recognize or authenticate anyone.
  • The App has no feature that uploads photos to our servers or to the ad SDK. Face photos are kept only in device memory while the result is shown and are not saved as a file. Palm photos are stored only in the App’s private storage on this device as a reduced copy (at most 720 pixels on the long side) and hand-position coordinates, so they can be shown again on the saved result screen. These files are not sent to our servers or to the ad SDK, and they are excluded from Android device backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • However, these two SDKs may send diagnostic and usage information, separate from photos, to Google (section 5).
  • Saving to the gallery and sharing happen only when you choose them, and share cards do not contain photos.

4. Lotto result updates

The App fetches the latest Lotto 6/45 winning numbers over HTTPS, without authentication, from two places:

  • Dong Haeng Lottery official site (Dong Haeng Lottery Co., Ltd., dhlottery.co.kr): the App requests the public winning numbers for a draw using only its round number (e.g. https://www.dhlottery.co.kr/lt645/selectPstLt645Info.do?srchLtEpsd=1250). It checks when you open the App, when you open the lotto screen, when you tap “check results again” (‘결과 다시 확인’), and during a background check your device runs about every six hours (only while a draw result is pending). On Saturdays from 20:35 to 23:00 (KST) it also checks once a minute while the lotto screen is open. The once-a-minute check stops when you leave the screen or the App goes to the background.
  • A fixed address on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): a fallback used when the official request fails or its format changes; the App downloads a results file.

In both cases the App does not send your name, date of birth, inputs, numbers generated in the App, advertising ID or cookies, and it does not store cookies set by those servers. Numbers are shown only after checking that the six numbers and the bonus number are between 1 and 45 without duplicates and that the round and draw date match.

However, as with any internet connection, your IP address, device/OS information (User-Agent) and the request time reach Dong Haeng Lottery and GitHub, and GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not receive or view these logs; their retention follows each company’s policies.

Dong Haeng Lottery privacy policy: https://www.dhlottery.co.kr (“개인정보처리방침” at the bottom of the site)
About GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages
GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

5. Information processed by Google SDKs

The following is as described in each SDK’s official documentation; processing and retention of this information follow the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

  • Google AdMob (ads) and User Messaging Platform (UMP, consent): Google states that AdMob automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, the IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions such as app launches and taps, app and SDK performance diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, encrypted in transit with TLS (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). UMP checks whether consent is required and stores your choice on the device. The App does not pass your profile, photos or results to the ad SDK. No ads are shown if you purchased ad removal or while you use Undam Plus. How Google uses information from partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google ML Kit (face detection): Google states that it sends device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version), per-installation identifiers not intended to identify a user or device, performance metrics, API configuration (such as image format and resolution), input and output size, feature version, event type and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics over HTTPS, and does not transfer them to third parties (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).
  • Google MediaPipe (hand landmarks): Google states that input data such as images is processed on the device and not sent to Google servers, and that performance and usage metrics (such as session and inference counts, hardware performance, app ID, general characteristics of processed media, and host system and version) are sent to Google to measure performance, improve the APIs and detect misuse (https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/legal/tos).
  • Google Play: Payments for Remove Ads (one-time) and Undam Plus (monthly subscription, which may include a 7-day free trial), renewals, cancellations and refunds, app update prompts and downloads of the App’s artwork (Play Asset Delivery) are handled through Google Play. We do not receive card or other payment details. The App checks purchase and subscription status on the device with Google Play Billing and does not send the result to us (we run no server). You can cancel or change your payment method in Google Play subscriptions.

6. Notifications

Fortune and lotto notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device; no information is sent to us for them. You can turn them off in the App. The notification permission is used only if you allow it.

  • If you use Undam Plus or purchased Remove Ads before 00:00 KST on November 14, 2026, the morning notification can include a one-line reading, lucky color and lucky numbers, all generated on the device.
  • If you are in an Undam Plus free trial with auto-renew on, the App schedules one local billing reminder around 9 a.m. two days before the first charge. It is not sent if you cancelled or have already been charged, and if notifications are off the same notice appears on the App’s home screen. No information is sent to us for this.
  • Saturday lotto purchase reminder: if turned on, the App schedules a local notification on Saturdays at the time you choose (3 p.m. by default; any hour from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.) saying “get this week’s numbers ready” (‘이번 주 번호 준비’) with a note that sales close at 8 p.m. You can turn it off on the lotto screen or in settings.
  • Lotto result notification: the App compares the lotto numbers you saved (including multi-game sets and favorite sets) with the official winning numbers fetched under section 4, on your device, and notifies you of the rank only after the result is confirmed. Your saved numbers and the comparison results are not sent to us or to Dong Haeng Lottery.

7. Purposes

  • Providing App features such as fortune, saju, tarot, palm, face, talisman and lotto-number generation and keeping your history
  • Showing and measuring ads and preventing fraud (AdMob)
  • Verifying in-app purchases and the Undam Plus subscription and providing its features (Google Play)
  • Billing reminder before a free trial ends (local notification)
  • Updating lotto results — public winning numbers from Dong Haeng Lottery, with a GitHub Pages results file as fallback (section 4)
  • Responding to inquiries (if you email us)

8. Sharing with third parties

Other than the services in sections 4 and 5, we do not provide or sell your information to third parties, except where required by law.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Information stored on the device stays there until you delete it. “Delete all local data” (‘모든 로컬 데이터 삭제’) on the “My info” (‘내 정보’) screen deletes your profile, history, settings, palm results and saved palm photos, and “Delete palm photos & results” (‘손금 사진·결과 삭제’) on the palm screen deletes palm photos and results only. Uninstalling the App also deletes the information inside it. Because there are no accounts, there is no separate account-deletion procedure.
  • Without Undam Plus, archive records older than 30 days are locked, not deleted, and reopen with Plus. You can still delete locked records with “Delete item” (‘항목 삭제’) or “Delete all records” (‘기록 모두 지우기’). Cancelling Plus does not remove your records or saved compatibility partners from the device.
  • Information included in Google backup can be managed in your device’s Google backup settings.
  • Images saved to your gallery or shared to other apps must be deleted there.
  • We keep no user data on our servers. Inquiry emails you send us are kept for one year after the inquiry is resolved and then deleted. To request earlier deletion, write to the same address (yu815kr@gmail.com).
  • Retention of information processed by Google, GitHub or Dong Haeng Lottery follows each company’s policies.

10. Advertising ID and personalized ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in device settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Menu names may vary by device and OS version. If UMP determines that consent is required, a consent form is shown when the App starts; if it determines that privacy options must be offered, “Ad privacy settings” (‘광고 개인정보 설정’) appears under “My info” (‘내 정보’) so you can change your choice.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

All readings and lotto-number generation in the App are for entertainment and reference only and are not medical, legal or investment advice. Lotto-number generation does not guarantee winning, and the App does not sell lottery tickets.
