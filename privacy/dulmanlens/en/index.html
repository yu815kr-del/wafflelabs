WaffleLabs (“we”) operates the Dulman Lens app (the “App”) and describes only the information the App actually processes.

1. Information stored on our servers (only if you use Pair mode)

If you do not pair, nothing is written to the server. Pair mode uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Seoul, asia-northeast3, project dulmanlens). We do not collect your name, email, or phone number. Only a randomly created anonymous user ID (uid) is created.

2. Information stored only on this device

  • Photos and videos you take (in an encrypted app vault using an Android Keystore key), encrypted chat history, your presets, lock settings, an intruder photo if you turn that option on (one front-camera still, on this device only), pair private and shared keys (wrapped with Keystore), and the reason the last call ended (wrapped with the pair key and cleared on unpair).
  • Face detection for beauty adjustment (ML Kit) runs on the device only. The App does not store or send face data. ML Kit is a Google library; any technical or usage data Google may process is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer (allowBackup=false and data-extraction rules that exclude backup and transfer).
  • Originals you receive are saved to the phone album folder “둘만의렌즈”. Because they live in the system album, they are not deleted when you unpair or uninstall the App. You can delete them in the gallery.

3. Original photos, videos, and calls (not stored on our servers)

  • Original photos and videos, and audio/video calls, travel directly between the two phones (WebRTC P2P). We do not upload originals to our servers. Firebase Storage is not used.
  • Traffic between the App and Firebase is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). The content of chat, thumbnails, and connection/call signals is end-to-end encrypted with a pair key known only to the two phones (X25519 key exchange + HKDF → AES-GCM). The server, including the operator, cannot read that content.
  • Original files are encrypted again with the pair key, in 16 KB chunks with AES-GCM, over WebRTC (DTLS), and checked with SHA-256.
  • Call media uses standard WebRTC encryption (DTLS-SRTP) between the phones.
  • If the other person is offline, the sender keeps the file in an encrypted on-device queue and sends it when both are online.
  • To help the phones find each other, the App uses Google’s public STUN servers (stun.l.google.com:19302, stun1.l.google.com:19302). Those servers see the device’s public IP address and port in order to assist connectivity; they do not see the content. No TURN (relay) server is used.
  • Because the connection is direct, the other phone can also learn your IP address.

4. Notifications

The App does not use FCM (push). There is no firebase-messaging library and no push token. While the App is open, updates arrive in real time from Firestore. While it is closed, WorkManager checks about every 15 minutes and shows a local notification (“it may be up to 15 minutes late”; battery savers may delay this further). Notification text does not include message bodies. If the recipient’s App is closed, a call will not ring; only a missed-call notification appears.

7. Your rights and deletion

  • Unpair in the App deletes the server pair record and all of its subcollections (messages, thumbnails, signaling, presence, and so on), and also deletes chat history and pair keys on both phones. The Firebase anonymous account remains.
  • App data on this device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data (backups are excluded). Originals already saved to the “둘만의렌즈” album remain.
  • If you uninstall without unpairing first, the server pair record may remain until the other person unpairs or you ask us to delete it.
  • See the data deletion page for steps. You can also email yu815kr@gmail.com to access, correct, delete, or stop processing of your information.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS, WebRTC DTLS-SRTP), end-to-end encryption for chat, signals, and thumbnails, and Firestore security rules so that only the two paired users can read and write pair data. No internet transmission can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.
