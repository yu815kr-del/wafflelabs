Privacy Policy

App: Chukchuk Unit Converter (척척 단위 변환기) · Package: com.wafflelabs.unitconverter
Operator: WaffleLabs (individual developer Wonjae Yu)
Effective date: 2026-11-20

The App’s screens are in Korean; Korean menu names are shown in parentheses.

WaffleLabs (“we”) explains below how information is handled in the app “Chukchuk Unit Converter” (the “App”) published on Google Play.

1. Accounts

The App has no sign-up or login, and we do not create or manage user accounts. We do not operate our own server to which the App sends user information.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Favorites (up to 30), recent conversions (last 20), home category order and values used to sort by most used
  • Sizes saved to “My size card”
  • Settings (decimal places, thousands separator, theme, occasional reminders on/off)
  • Ad-frequency records and the result of the ad-removal purchase check

The App does not send this information to us. If Android backup (Google backup) is turned on, favorites, history and settings may be included in your backup. The ad-removal purchase check result is excluded from backup and is checked again with Google Play when the App starts. All conversions are calculated on the device, and the values you enter are never sent off it (the App does not fetch exchange rates or other online data).

3. Notifications

Only if you turn on occasional reminders (‘가끔 알려 주기’, off by default) in Settings, the App sends a return reminder at most once a week, and only between 8 AM and 9 PM. On Android 13 and later this needs notification (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) permission; you can decline and still use other features. Reminders are scheduled on the device with Android WorkManager and send no information off it. The App does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

4. Information processed by Google SDKs

The following is as described in each SDK’s official documentation; processing and retention of this information follow the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Google AdMob (ads) and User Messaging Platform (UMP, consent): The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the UMP SDK and uses the advertising ID (AD_ID) permission. Google states that AdMob automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, the IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions such as app launches, taps, ad impressions and clicks, app and SDK performance diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, encrypted in transit (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). UMP checks whether consent is required in your region and stores your choice on the device. The current version has no ad units configured, so it does not request or show ads; however, some of the information above may be sent to Google when the App checks whether consent is required and initializes the SDK at launch, and ads may be shown in a future update. The App does not pass your conversion inputs or history to the ad SDK. If you purchase ad removal, the App does not request ads. How Google uses information from partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Play: The one-time ad-removal purchase and update prompts (Play In-App Updates) are handled by Google Play. Google processes payment details; we never receive your card number or other payment information. The App checks purchase status on the device with the Google Play Billing Library and does not send the result to us.

5. Send feedback and Recommend to a friend

“Send feedback” opens your email app with only the app version and Android version prefilled. We receive the message and your email address only if you send it, and use them only to read and reply. “Recommend to a friend” opens the Android share sheet with a short intro and the Google Play link; the App does not read your contacts or recipients. There are no rewards.

6. Purposes

  • Providing App features and keeping settings and history (on the device)
  • Showing and measuring ads and preventing fraud (AdMob, in versions with ads turned on)
  • Verifying the ad-removal purchase (Google Play)
  • Responding to inquiries (if you email us)

7. Sharing with third parties

Other than the Google services in section 4, we do not provide or sell your information to third parties, except where required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

Information stored on the device stays there until you delete it. You can clear recent conversions with “Clear history” (‘기록 지우기’) and remove favorites individually, and delete all App data via Android Settings → Apps → 척척 단위 변환기 (Chukchuk Unit Converter) → Storage → “Clear data”, or by uninstalling the App. Because there are no accounts, there is no separate account-deletion procedure. Information included in Google backup can be managed in your device’s Google backup settings.

We keep no user data on our servers. Inquiry emails you send us are kept for one year after the inquiry is resolved and then deleted. To request earlier deletion, write to the same address (yu815kr@gmail.com). Retention of information processed by Google follows Google’s policies.

9. Advertising ID and personalized ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Menu names may vary by device and OS version. If UMP determines that consent is required, a consent form appears when the App starts, and in regions where privacy options are required, an “Ad consent settings” (‘광고 동의 설정’) item appears in the App’s settings so you can change your choice.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

11. Notice on results

Conversion results and size charts are for reference. Sizes can differ by brand, so check the brand’s own size chart before buying. For trade, contracts or other important uses, also check the official standards.

12. Security

We take reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post it on this page with the effective date and announce significant changes in the App or store listing.
