WaffleLabs (“we”) respects the privacy of users of our Google Play mobile applications (“Apps”). This policy applies to Apps published under the WaffleLabs developer name.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the App, we may process:

  • Device and app data (model, OS version, app version, locale) for stability and improvement
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID) when ads are shown; users may reset or opt out in device settings
  • Usage analytics and crash logs (e.g. Firebase) when enabled
  • Information you send us (e.g. support emails)

We do not require national ID numbers, bank account numbers, or similar sensitive credentials in our Apps.

Microphone (recording): The App requests the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) permission only when you start a recording in “My sounds”. Recordings are stored only on your device; they are never sent to our servers or any third party and are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete recordings in the App at any time, and they are removed when the App is uninstalled.

  • Respond to support requests

4. Retention

We retain data only as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. SDK retention follows the respective provider policies.

5. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. You can reset or limit advertising ID use in your device settings.

6. Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13. If we publish an App directed at children, we will provide additional notices and required parental consent flows.
